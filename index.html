About Envento

Envento is the event management club of IIIT Vadodara, Gandhinagar Campus. We plan, coordinate and run events for the student community.

The club's work is summarised each year in the Envento Annual Report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Envento?

Any student at IIIT Vadodara, Gandhinagar Campus, can join. No prior experience is required.

How do I register for an event?

Use the Register button on each event page, or fill out the join form below.
